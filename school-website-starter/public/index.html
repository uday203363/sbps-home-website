About Our School

Rooted in Tradition, Soaring in Innovation

"Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire."

Providing Quality Education in Kadapa Since 2006

Sri Bhashyam Public School has emerged as a beacon of academic rigor and moral values in Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh. Inspired by our rich heritage, our school name honors wisdom and the pursuit of truth. Our school emblem shows the Gurukul lineage: learning under the shades of the sacred banyan tree, symbolizing growth, support, and wisdom.

We are recognized by the Government of Andhra Pradesh (AP State Board), offering comprehensive curriculum structures that spark curiosity and develop lifelong learning skills in both primary and high school students.
