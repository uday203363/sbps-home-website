Our Academic Offerings

At Sri Bhashyam, we prioritize a holistic curriculum designed to foster deep understanding, critical inquiry, and competitive rigor. We blend Montessori concepts in primary classes with AP State Board frameworks in secondary tiers to create an optimal balance of success.

Teaching Methodology & Departments

Experiential Pedagogy

We actively steer away from rote memorization. Our concepts are delivered using interactive visual slides, hands-on lab experiments, science model constructions, and collaborative group discussions.

Core Academic Departments

  • Sciences Wing: Specialized lab-oriented training in Physics, Chemistry, and Biological Sciences.
  • Mathematics: Logical deduction frameworks and advanced competitive mental math.
  • Languages: Complete communication fluency in English, Telugu, and Hindi.
  • Social Studies: Civic awareness and local/global history timelines.

Examination & Evaluations

We host rigorous evaluations aligned with state norms to prepare students for peak success. Our testing patterns include:

  • Weekly Formative Slip Assessments.
  • Quarterly and Half-Yearly Summative Evaluations.
  • Specialized State Board mock exams for Class IX and X.
  • Weekly Mock tests for IIT-JEE/NEET aspirants.

Academic Calendar & Co-Curriculars

Co-Curricular Activities

Academic learning is incomplete without physical and creative outlets. We offer structured training programs in:

  • Creative Arts: Classical/modern dance, paintings, and crafts.
  • Public Speaking: Model United Nations simulations, inter-school debates, and quiz bowls.
  • Sports: Physical training in Cricket, Volley Ball, Badminton, Chess, and Karate.
