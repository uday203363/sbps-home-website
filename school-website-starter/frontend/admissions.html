Eligibility Criteria & Guidelines

Age Criteria

  • Nursery / LKG: Child must have completed 3 years of age as of June 1st of the academic year.
  • Class I: Child must have completed 5 years of age as of June 1st of the academic year.
  • Classes II to X: Valid Transfer Certificate (TC) and previous academic report card required.

Required Documents

  • Original Birth Certificate copy.
  • Aadhaar card copy of student and parent.
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from recognized schools (Class II onwards).
  • 3 passport-sized recent photos.

Admissions FAQs

Is there an admission test?

For Nursery and Primary classes, we only conduct a friendly interaction session. Grades VI to X will undergo a basic readiness assessment test in English, Math, and Science.

Can we pay tuition fees in installments?

Yes. Tuition fees can be remitted in three installments across the academic year (June, October, and January).

Are buses available to all areas in Kadapa?

Yes, our transport system spans all prime locations inside municipal limits with regular tracking updates.
